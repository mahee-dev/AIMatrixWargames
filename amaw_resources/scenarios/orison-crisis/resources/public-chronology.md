# Public Crisis Chronology

## Six months before the crisis

Relations between the Republic of Ardin and the Commonwealth of Orison deteriorate
after a failed regional diplomatic conference. Ardin publicly states that it opposes
the expansion of Vesper military power into the Meridian Sea.

Orison accuses Ardin of supporting exile organizations seeking to remove its current
government. Ardin denies planning an invasion.

## Three months before the crisis

Vesper and Orison announce an expanded defensive-cooperation agreement. The published
statement refers to training, air defence, engineering, and military assistance. It
does not disclose long-range strategic weapons.

## Five weeks before Turn 1

Commercial observers report unusually heavy Vesper shipping to Orison. Vesper describes
the shipments as defensive assistance and infrastructure material.

## Eight days before Turn 1

Ardin reconnaissance detects construction patterns at multiple sites on Orison that
analysts associate with long-range missile support facilities.

Ardin begins additional reconnaissance.

## Three days before Turn 1

New imagery provides strong evidence that Vesper strategic weapons and supporting
equipment are present on Orison. Some sites remain incomplete.

Ardin's government begins emergency deliberations.

## Turn 1 opening

Each game turn represents approximately one crisis day. Construction, military readiness, diplomatic pressure, and shipping decisions may therefore change materially over the six-turn game even without a scripted escalation track.


Ardin has not attacked Orison and has not imposed a maritime interdiction.

Vesper has not announced withdrawal.

Orison has not accepted foreign inspection of the sites.

No trilateral settlement exists.

## Existing defensive installation

Orison also hosts Meridian Watch, a Vesper-operated early-warning and maritime-tracking station. Publicly available information describes it as non-strategic and distinct from the newly discovered missile deployment.
