# Joint Planning Staff Estimate
## Limited Air Operation Against Known Orison Sites

**Distribution:** Ardin leadership only

A concentrated air operation could probably destroy or seriously damage a large share
of the strategic infrastructure currently identified by reconnaissance.

It cannot guarantee destruction of every strategic weapon or supporting component.

## Main uncertainties

1. Intelligence may not identify every storage or launch location.
2. Some equipment could move before or during an operation.
3. Damage assessment would initially be incomplete.
4. Vesper and Orison air-defence activity could cause losses and broaden the fight.
5. Vesper personnel casualties could change Vesper's willingness to escalate.
6. A limited operation could create pressure for follow-on attacks if initial results
   are ambiguous.

## Ground follow-on

Military planners assess that physically securing every suspect site would require a
much larger operation than the initial strikes.

Such an operation would create direct combat with Orison forces and possibly Vesper
personnel.

## Planning conclusion

Military action is feasible.

A claim that it guarantees complete removal of the strategic threat is not supported
by this estimate.
