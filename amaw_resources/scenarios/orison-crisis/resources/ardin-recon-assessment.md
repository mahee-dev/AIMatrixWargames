# Directorate of Strategic Assessment
## Reconnaissance Estimate: Orison Deployment

**Distribution:** Ardin leadership only

### High-confidence findings

Repeated overhead imagery shows construction at three military areas consistent with
long-range missile support and launch preparation.

Large cylindrical transporters and associated support vehicles have been observed at
two sites.

Vesper military personnel are present.

### Moderate-confidence findings

At least one site could plausibly become operational within several days if work
continues without interruption.

Some equipment may be stored in covered areas not continuously visible to current
reconnaissance.

The visible sites probably do not represent every Vesper military activity on Orison.

### Unknowns

- Exact number of strategic weapons already on Orison.
- Whether warheads are stored separately or already co-located with delivery systems.
- Exact operational readiness of each site.
- Whether mobile or concealed systems exist outside the known areas.
- Local rules governing use of defensive weapons against an Ardin attack.

### Analytic warning

The imagery strongly supports the conclusion that a strategic deployment exists.

It does **not** establish that an Ardin strike could find or destroy every weapon, nor
does it establish Vesper's political intention to launch them.

## Time pressure

Each game turn represents approximately one crisis day. If construction continues, delaying several turns can therefore materially change Ardin's assessment of readiness. This does not mean readiness advances automatically by a fixed rule; it means Arguments and adjudication should treat elapsed turns as relevant evidence.
