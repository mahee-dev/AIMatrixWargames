# Maritime and Sovereignty Memorandum

This memorandum is authoritative scenario background, not real-world legal advice.

## Territorial sovereignty

Orison controls access to military sites and territorial facilities on Orison.
A foreign state has no standing permission to inspect or occupy those sites.

Vesper's presence is based on Orison's existing consent.

Either Orison or Vesper may alter commitments that are within its own authority, but
neither can substitute for a required decision by the other.

## International waters

Outside recognized territorial waters, states may operate naval vessels and conduct
observation subject to the crisis rules and ordinary navigation rights.

## Interdiction

A general attempt to stop or divert foreign shipping is a major coercive act. Calling
such an operation a "quarantine", "inspection zone", or "safety measure" does not by
itself determine its legal or political consequences.

Whether a particular operation successfully stops a vessel is an operational question.
Whether another state accepts the underlying demand is a separate political question.

## Inspection and verification

Inspection of sites on Orison requires Orison's consent unless control of those sites
has been changed by an authoritative game Effect.

Vesper may disclose information about Vesper-owned weapons, but disclosure does not
automatically create physical inspection rights.

## Agreements

A public statement of support is not automatically a binding trilateral agreement.
Where a proposed arrangement requires commitments from multiple Actors, those
commitments must already exist or be established by the relevant Actors.
