# Regional Geography Note

The accompanying GeoJSON map is schematic and **not to scale**.

Use these qualitative relationships when reasoning about geography:

- Orison lies close to the Republic of Ardin and within the main operating range of Ardin regional air and naval forces.
- Vesper's mainland lies substantially farther from Orison than Ardin does.
- Talmera is an Ardin-allied state located near Vesper territory, far from Orison.
- Vesper must sustain forces on Orison across a longer logistical route than Ardin needs to reach the immediate crisis area.
- Meridian Watch is a fixed early-warning installation on Orison. Its existence is public; the exact locations of the newly discovered strategic missile sites are **not** public scenario information.

Do not infer real-world distances, travel times, weapon ranges, or probabilities from the map coordinates.
