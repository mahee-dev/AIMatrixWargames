# Meridian Watch Technical Note

Meridian Watch is a fixed Vesper-operated early-warning and maritime-tracking installation on Orison.

It predates the current strategic missile deployment.

## Publicly established characteristics

- It is hosted with Orison's consent.
- Its declared mission is long-range warning, airspace observation, and maritime tracking.
- It is not itself a strategic missile site.
- It can operate without the newly deployed strategic weapons.
- Its sensors provide Vesper and Orison with useful regional warning information.

## Areas of uncertainty and disagreement

The public record does **not** establish exactly which sensor modes, data-processing functions,
or remote data links are active at all times.

Ardin argues that a sufficiently capable warning station could also collect militarily useful
information about Ardin forces.

Vesper argues that detailed inspection of sensor architecture, software, or data links could
expose legitimate military technology and intelligence methods unrelated to the missile crisis.

Orison values the warning function but is concerned that future upgrades could expand the mission
without adequate host-state oversight.

## Political significance

Ardin considers the station an unwelcome extension of Vesper military presence but historically
has treated it as less threatening than strategic offensive weapons.

Orison values the warning function because it contributes to its sense of protection against surprise attack.

Vesper values the station independently of the strategic deployment.

## Verification possibilities

Because Meridian Watch is fixed and has a narrower declared mission than the strategic missile
deployment, the Actors could plausibly negotiate limits, transparency measures, equipment
restrictions, inspections, upgrade notifications, data-use constraints, or shared observation.

No single verification design is obviously acceptable to all three Actors:

- more intrusive inspection gives Ardin greater confidence but may expose Vesper technology;
- weak inspection protects Vesper secrets but may not reassure Ardin;
- one-time inspection may not address future upgrades that concern Orison;
- physical access alone may not reveal how remote data transmission is configured.

No such arrangement currently exists.

Whether any proposed arrangement is politically acceptable still requires the relevant Actors' consent.
