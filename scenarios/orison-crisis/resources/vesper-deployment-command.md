# Presidium Security Memorandum
## Orison Deployment: Political Purpose and Command Concerns

**Distribution:** Vesper leadership only

The deployment was approved for two linked purposes:

1. strengthen deterrence against a future Ardin attack on Orison; and
2. improve Vesper's strategic position relative to forward Ardin capabilities near
   Vesper territory.

The operation was not authorized as preparation for a first strike against Ardin.

## Political guidance

A major war with Ardin is to be avoided.

If Ardin provides a credible non-attack arrangement for Orison and other strategically
meaningful concessions, withdrawal may be considered.

A reciprocal Ardin change concerning its forward strategic systems in Talmera would
have substantial value. Public presentation matters: leadership strongly prefers that
any Talmera change not be announced as a direct forced exchange.

## Alliance limitation

Orison is an ally, not Vesper territory.

Vesper can order withdrawal or disposition of Vesper-owned weapons and personnel.

Vesper cannot validly promise foreign inspection of Orison facilities without Orison's
agreement.

## Command concern

Local commanders possess defensive capabilities intended to protect the deployment and
Vesper personnel. Central leadership expects political control of major escalation,
but crisis communications may be delayed or incomplete.

Do not assume that every local tactical event will be immediately known in the capital.

## Separate Vesper interest: Meridian Watch

Vesper also values the existing Meridian Watch early-warning station on Orison. It is not part of the strategic missile deployment.

Preserving its defensive warning function is independently important to Vesper. However, Vesper considers some sensor architecture, software, communications pathways, and data-processing methods sensitive. A verification regime that effectively discloses those capabilities could impose costs unrelated to the missile dispute.

Vesper therefore has an interest in preserving both the station's warning value and reasonable protection for sensitive technology.
