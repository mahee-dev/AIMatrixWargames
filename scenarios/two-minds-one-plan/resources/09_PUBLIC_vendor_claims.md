# Official product bids: contradictory marketing claims
**PUBLIC.** Aster's deck says: "Our source-priority hierarchy remained robust in published tests." Meridian's says: "MIRROR-B demonstrated superior safety performance in 120 comparative cases." Both statements may be defensible only with important qualifications. Neither demonstrates actual production incidents or human injury.

The procurement body controls the final decision; the laboratories control their submissions and explanations. The AI agents have no corporate signing authority. A market commentator estimates the winning supplier will receive an early tranche of payment, but that is not a legally binding financing contract.

At 48:00 the reader should be able to ask an empirically meaningful question: Which claims were corrected, which remained unsupported, and who knew what before making a public commitment?
