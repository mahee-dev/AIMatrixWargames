# Aster Board restricted record — financing and accountability
**Aster only.** Contract exposure: €1.8bn over seven years, approximately 22% of forecast revenue. Liquidity bridge: €140m covenant review at fictional hour 36. Adverse disclosure could require a waiver; it does not make Aster insolvent automatically. A short lawful deferment is possible but the lender has not agreed to it.

The ORA-17 safety lead sent the board a signed triage request before the crisis; a committee deferred classification pending replication. Neither the executive chair nor counsel approved misrepresenting the finding. The public bid deck nevertheless states "no identified material source-priority anomalies".

Legal counsel warns: a delayed but truthful correction may preserve credibility if accompanied by an independent comparison; a denial contradicted by signed internal evidence threatens long-term bidder eligibility. The CEO's incentive to protect the company and the safety officer's duty to report are in conflict, even without a villain.

**Board leverage:** you can permit a confidential clean-room comparison, disclose and negotiate a finance waiver, postpone the award, or defend a narrow description of the issue. You cannot compel Meridian or hide escrow records.
