# Campaign concept — Eastern Sea (restricted)

**Aim:** show Vardland's population and its allies that Skarö cannot be held without costly
foreign help, and that such help brings danger rather than safety.

**Lines of effort**

1. **Presence.** Keep the exercise visible and lawful. Stay in international waters and
   airspace. Close approaches are acceptable. Crossing into territory is not.
2. **Narrative.** Amplify government-failure stories. Frame any Vardland reinforcement or
   Aldmoor role as militarisation that endangers islanders.
3. **Offers.** Offer civilian help, such as a repair vessel or humanitarian goods. Acceptance
   shows Vardland's dependence, and refusal can be framed as putting politics over people.
4. **Wedges.** Exploit the difference between the national government and island interests,
   and between Vardland and Aldmoor.

**Constraints:** no action that Aldmoor could credibly call an armed attack; deniability for
anything below that line; avoid giving Vardland evidence it could use for attribution.

**Risk:** if Vardland publishes credible evidence about the survey vessel, the campaign's
narrative collapses and Aldmoor's domestic resistance to deployment weakens.
