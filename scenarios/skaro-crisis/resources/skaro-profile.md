# Skarö county profile

- **Population:** about 38,000, mostly in the port town of Skarö Hamn. Numbers rise in summer
  and fall in winter.
- **Geography:** a low limestone island in the Eastern Sea, about eight hours by ferry from the
  Vardland mainland. Drevnian territory is closer to Skarö than the Vardland mainland is.
- **Energy:** normally supplied by one subsea power and data cable from the mainland. Local wind
  turbines cannot run the island's grid without the cable connection. Backup diesel generation
  covers essential services, not full normal consumption.
- **Transport:** one ferry port (also the cable landing site) and one airfield able to take
  medium transport aircraft.
- **Health:** one county hospital. Serious cases are normally flown to the mainland.
- **Military presence:** a small permanent garrison. It was re-established a few years ago
  after a long absence, and some residents still debate it.
- **Economy:** tourism, fishing, agriculture and a small data-centre operation that has been
  offline since the cable failed.
