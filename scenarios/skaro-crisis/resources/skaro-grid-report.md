# Skarö grid operator report to the County Administration (confidential)

- **Fault location:** approximately 18 km from the Skarö landing, in water about 60 m deep.
- **Fault signature:** a sudden break in both the power and the fibre cores at the same point,
  consistent with an external mechanical force such as an anchor or a trawled object. It is not
  consistent with gradual wear.
- **Repair:** needs a specialised cable-repair vessel, good weather and unobstructed access to
  the fault area. Once the vessel is on site, repair and testing take at least a day.
- **Backup generation:** the primary units are old. The operator estimates a real risk that at
  least one unit fails in the coming week under continuous load.
- **Priority loads:** hospital, water and sewage, the county crisis centre, heating for the
  warming centres, the radio link.
