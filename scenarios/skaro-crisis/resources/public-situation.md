# Public situation report — Skarö

*Compiled from public statements and open reporting. Fictional.*

## Day 0 (evening)

- The Skarö grid operator reports a total loss of power and data transfer on the subsea cable
  linking Skarö to the Vardland mainland. Backup generation starts automatically at the
  hospital and the water plant, and island-wide within four hours.
- Mobile networks on Skarö run on backup power. Internet capacity is sharply reduced and relies
  on a narrow radio link.

## Day 1

- The Drevnian Ministry of Defence confirms that its announced autumn naval and air exercise
  has begun in international waters south-east of Skarö. It says the exercise is routine and
  unconnected to "any technical failures in Vardland".
- The Skarö County Administration activates its crisis organisation, opens two warming centres
  and asks residents to limit electricity use.
- Posts claiming that the Vardland government "let the cable rot" gain wide circulation.
- The Vardland Prime Minister says the cause of the failure is unknown and that "all
  possibilities are being examined".
- Aldmoor's foreign minister says Aldmoor "stands ready to help its partner in any way
  Vardland wishes".

## Day 2 (start of play)

- Ferry operators report unusually high demand for departures from Skarö.
- Drevna's embassy offers "humanitarian and technical assistance" to the people of Skarö.
- No official finding on the cause of the cable failure has been published.
