## Observations

- Official county updates are trusted by island residents but reach few people outside Skarö.
- Reduced internet capacity on Skarö means many residents now get their news from radio and
  from relatives on the mainland.
- Any visible military activity on the island is likely to be framed online as a "takeover",
  whoever it serves.
