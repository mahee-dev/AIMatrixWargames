# Political assessment for the crisis cabinet (restricted)

- **Coalition.** The junior coalition partner will accept heightened alert only with a clear
  public justification, such as an evidenced threat or a failure of civil means. A declaration
  that looks like a reflex will split the government.
- **Public mood.** Mainland opinion favours firmness. Island opinion favours visible help with
  power and heat, and is sceptical of soldiers on the streets.
- **Alliance.** Accepting Aldmoor surveillance is broadly popular. Aldmoor ground forces on
  Skarö are not, and would dominate Drevnian messaging.
- **Attribution.** Evidence available to the Cabinet so far shows a survey vessel near the
  cable route, but not what it did. An attribution on this basis would be contested abroad.
- **Budget.** Sustained fuel shipments and generator deployments beyond two weeks require an
  extraordinary appropriation, which the opposition will use to question the response.
