# Vardland total-defence framework — summary for decision-makers

*Fictional scenario law. The binding points are restated as the scenario rules; this note
explains the context.*

## Principles

Vardland organises its defence as **total defence**: military defence and civil defence are
two parts of one national effort. In peacetime and in grey-zone crises, civil authorities lead
on civilian matters, and the armed forces support them on request.

Three principles guide the response:

1. **Responsibility.** The authority responsible for an activity in normal conditions keeps
   it in a crisis.
2. **Proximity.** A crisis is handled at the lowest level able to handle it. On Skarö, that is
   the County Administration.
3. **Similarity.** Organisation and working methods change as little as possible.

## Alert levels

The Cabinet may declare **heightened alert** (partial, for named regions or functions, or
full). Heightened alert is a political signal as well as a legal switch. It allows reservist
call-up and the movement of forces without further decisions, and it gives the Civil
Contingencies Agency cross-county powers over supplies. It does not make the armed forces
responsible for civilian protection.

## Foreign forces

Foreign forces may be present on Vardland territory, in its waters or in its airspace only
with a Cabinet decision. That decision can be narrow, for example "surveillance flights over
Vardland waters for fourteen days", and it can be withdrawn.

## Attribution

The Cabinet alone speaks for Vardland on who is responsible for a hostile act. Agencies
provide evidence and assessments. Attributing an act to a state has diplomatic and legal
consequences, including under the alliance treaty with Aldmoor.

## The alliance treaty with Aldmoor

In the event of an armed attack on either party, the other will consult "without delay" on
the assistance it deems necessary. The treaty does not define grey-zone activity, sabotage
that cannot be attributed, or cyber disruption as an armed attack.
