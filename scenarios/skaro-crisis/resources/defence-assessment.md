# Defence Command situation assessment (restricted)

**Confidence:** mixed. These are judgements, not facts.

1. **Cable.** Naval surveillance recorded a Drevnian-contracted survey vessel moving slowly
   along the cable route about twelve hours before the failure. The recording shows position
   and speed only. *Assessment: sabotage plausible; not proven.*
2. **Exercise.** The Drevnian exercise is larger than last year's and includes landing ships.
   *Assessment: most likely signalling; preparation for a limited seizure of Skarö cannot be
   ruled out and would be hard to reverse once it began.*
3. **Garrison.** The Skarö garrison can protect the airfield or the port and cable landing,
   but not both at once for long.
4. **Reinforcement.** The first units by air can arrive about one day after a decision, and
   heavier units by sea after about two days. Both need heightened alert, or a civil request
   limited to support tasks.
5. **Risk of over-reaction.** Visible reinforcement without a public justification would feed
   the "takeover" narrative on the island and abroad.
