# Cross-capital authenticated relay — terms of use
**Recipients:** Joint Cell, both national Leaders. **Not visible to either Command unless a Leader shares it.**

A neutral standby line last completed an end-to-end test three months ago. Consent of each Leader, a functioning communication path, and an acknowledged receipt are separate prerequisites. The cell can authenticate a sender but cannot verify the truth of that sender's statement. Delayed normal executive traffic is an independent problem, not proof of hostile interference.

An authentic Boran note could say: "Exercise confirmed defensive. We will provide a bounded contemporaneous description if Aster reciprocally describes defensive readiness actions." Aster could reject reciprocity or condition its response on independent evidence. The relay cannot compel either side to reveal sensitive operational details.

**Operator reminder:** AMAW diplomacy messages do not automatically count as an authenticated international relay. The Umpire should establish sender authentication only following a grounded, adjudicated communication action; statements of receipt must likewise be earned.
