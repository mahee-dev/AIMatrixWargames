# COUNTERSIGN / 09:00 — public crisis clock
**For every player. Fictional nations Aster and Boran.** Three turn windows, 00–03, 03–06 and 06–09 SIMULATED minutes. Each turn is a round of policy and command decisions, not literal simultaneous minute-by-minute software execution. Real provider latency is irrelevant.

Sentinel-S8 warns of a possible strategic attack. Human review disputes it. No physical launch or strategic release has been confirmed. Both sides have incentives to avoid war but believe **reversible defensive readiness** may be necessary. The public can observe alerts and vehicle movements without knowing what caused them.

National Leaders alone can authorize strategic release. Commands have narrower, established defensive-readiness authority; an alleged release is not execution. The game never models weapon targeting or technical employment. Neither side receives private intelligence automatically from the other. A raw AMAW Message is a claim; sender-authenticated external crisis traffic requires an established relay and receipt.

The nine-minute deadline is a cabinet review point: by 09:00 every government's actions will require political justification. It is NOT a mechanical forced-attack timer. What happens depends on what the actors actually choose.
