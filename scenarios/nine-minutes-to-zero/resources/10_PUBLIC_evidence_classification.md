# Public evidence labels for crisis reporting
An image of a convoy is proof that some vehicles were present at a recorded time, not proof of weapons, mission, current orders or future intent. A social graphic is evidence of what a system displayed, not a Leader's order. A signed message authenticates its sender, not every assertion inside it. Sensor non-detection within a narrow geographic sector is not a universal negative.

Useful words for exact reporting: **alleged**, **observed**, **authenticated sender**, **unverified purpose**, **corroborated**, **not corroborated**, **ordered**, and **implemented**. These are separate evidence classes.

Aster and Boran's responsibility is to decide under uncertainty. The scenario permits multiple end states, including calm restoration, enduring guarded readiness, a public rupture, and a severe authorization crisis. It does not presume that rational people always de-escalate or always panic.
