# Aster Strategic Command: reversible options sheet
**Aster Strategic Command and Leader.**

The current standing posture has no newly adjudicated change. Command officers believe three high-level options merit civilian discussion: (A) maintain while requesting independent confirmation, (B) take a limited reversible protection measure, (C) visibly elevate defensive readiness. All three have costs. The more visible the measure, the more likely Boran may mistake it for offensive preparation. Delaying protective readiness leaves existing vulnerabilities in place if the warning is unexpectedly genuine.

This sheet does NOT provide a targeting or execution procedure and conveys no permission for strategic weapon use. The Command can recommend; Leaders can direct within lawful authority. An actual change must be established in a game action, with the corresponding public or private visibility determined by evidence.

The most dangerous mistake is to conclude that 'reversible' means 'unobservable'. Boran may only learn the visible part, and may react before the benign intent can be authenticated.
